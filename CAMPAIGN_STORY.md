# RealmQuest: Beyond the Veil

## Premise
**Oakwood Vale** has been sealed inside **the Veil** for eleven years. The Veil is a shimmering barrier across the sky that no one can cross. Three **Veil Anchors** hold it up, and each is buried in a dungeon and guarded by a monster lord. The anchors also breed the creatures that **raid Oakwood every night**.

**Merlin the Great Sage** has protected the village all this time with a ward over the plaza, but he is old and his strength is fading. With the last of it he performed a summoning, and the hero **Himmel** wakes in the plaza's stone circle with no memory of anything before the light.

Merlin can't leave his ward, so he stays in Oakwood and guides Himmel from there. He gives Himmel a **Speaking Stone**, so his voice goes with him. Defeat the three bosses, shatter the three anchors, and the Veil falls.

## Campaign route
Three dungeons of three floors each. The third floor of each dungeon holds its boss and its anchor.

| Dungeon | Floors | Theme | Boss | Anchor | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Goblin Dungeon | 1-1 to 1-3 | Forest & Ruins | Goblin King (Valdur) | Anchor of Thorns | Berserker portal |
| Berserker Warriors Dungeon | 2-1 to 2-3 | Ancient Ruins | Minotaur | Anchor of Blood | Lich King portal |
| Lich King Dungeon | 3-1 to 3-3 | The Underworld | The Lich King | Anchor of Bone (the keystone) | The Veil falls |

**Each chapter goes:** defeat the boss → shatter the anchor where it fell → return to Oakwood for a story scene with Merlin and Maren. Once an anchor shatters, the raids from that dungeon stop.

On each dungeon's first and second floor, a **lore tablet** in the starting room reveals the plot one piece at a time:
- **Goblins:** the anchor calls the monsters out every night. An older carving reveals the Veil was built on purpose: "Three stones to hold the sky."
- **Berserkers:** a tally of 4,015 nights of raids, and the first mention of the **Veilwright**, who promised the monsters the valley forever.
- **Lich King:** "Two masters wove the Veil. One stayed to guard it. One stayed to watch it." Merlin admits he has been slow to tell the truth. A note signed "V." shows the Veilwright knows a hero has been summoned.

**The epilogue:** the sky turns plain blue for the first time in eleven years. Merlin confesses that he and the Veilwright wove the Veil together long ago, to hide the valley from a war. When the war ended, the Veilwright refused to let go. Merlin stayed behind to protect Oakwood from the cage he helped build.

## Threads for future content
- **The Veilwright** is still out there beyond the mountains, and now knows the cage is open.
- **Merlin's past:** who taught the Veilwright to weave barriers, and why he never left the valley. The Lich King hints at both.
- **The world outside:** no one in Oakwood has seen it in eleven years. Captain Hale is already gathering an expedition, and Pip has signed up three times.
- **Lisbet's husband, Aldric,** walked into the Veil eleven years ago. He may be somewhere on the other side.

## Dungeon floors
Every visit to a dungeon builds new floors.
- **Floors 1 and 2:** the stairs are behind a locked door. The **floor key** is held by a guard room (floor 1) or a mini-boss (floor 2) down a side branch.
- **Floor 3:** the boss chamber is sealed. Two seal rooms and a mini-boss each hold a **boss seal**, and all three open the gate.
- **Optional rooms** on every floor: vaults with big chests, shrines with a healing font, and spike-trap gauntlets.
- **The map draws itself** as you explore. Finding every room earns a gold bonus.

**Merlin in the dungeons:** he speaks through the Speaking Stone in the big moments: boss encounters, the anchors and the lore tablets.

## Growing stronger
The dungeon seals only yield to the strong: the Goblin Dungeon needs **level 5** (it suits levels 5-10), the Berserkers **level 15** (15-20) and the Lich King **level 20** (20-30; the level cap is 30). Himmel trains in the six **mini dungeons**, hunts the packs of the **Deep Forest**, burns **monster lairs**, and answers **world events** (a roaming champion, a caravan under attack). Merlin (talk to him in the plaza) suggests a cave that suits Himmel's level. Exploring the overworld gives only a little XP; the caves and dungeons are where Himmel really grows.

## Night raids
The anchors' creatures come every night. A raid plays out if Himmel is near Oakwood at nightfall (if he's away, the village watch holds):
1. **The horn sounds** and the monsters come down the road from the dungeon you're currently working on: goblins first, then Berserkers, then Deadlings.
2. **Merlin moves to the plaza** and holds his ward, a ring of blue runes. Every monster that reaches it weakens it.
3. **Meet them on the road** and strike with Space or a click. There are 2–3 waves.
4. **Win:** gold, and the night counts as defended. **Lose** (the ward breaks or Himmel falls): Merlin blasts the monsters away, Himmel wakes in the plaza, and the raid costs a little gold. There is no game over.

At night, red eyes watch from the woods, and things howl in the distance. Once all three anchors are gone, the nights are quiet.

## Quests
The main quest, *Beyond the Veil*, follows the route above. The villagers also have eight side quests (see the README for the full table). Several of them build the world:
- **The Old Wayshrines** (Maren): four standing stones tell how the Veil was raised to hide the valley from a war, how the two masters fell out, and how "a hero called from beyond" would one day break the stones.
- **Letters to the Lost** (Lisbet): the **Wall of Names**, built right against the Veil on the eastern overlook, lists everyone lost to the raids or to the Veil. Aldric's name is there.
- **The Missing Scouts** (Hale): three scouts who followed a raid back to its den.
- **Shards for the Ward** (the Farmstead Folk): ward-glass from the Lich's halls lets Merlin stretch his ward over the outlying farms.

## Oakwood Village
| Character | Role |
|---|---|
| Merlin the Great Sage | Guards the ward by the plaza; talk to him for advice. Speaks to Himmel through the Speaking Stone when he's away |
| Elder Maren | Village elder; story scenes after each chapter |
| Torvin the Blacksmith | Shop: sword damage, armor (max HP), Windstep Boots (faster dash) |
| Wren the Herbalist | Shop: healing potions, Mending Herbs (stronger potions) |
| Captain Hale | Leads the night watch; tactical hints |
| Pip, Old Bram, Lisbet | Villagers whose lines change as the story moves on |
| Farmstead Folk | Families burned out of the outlying farms (after dungeon 2) |

Every villager has new lines after each chapter. Over their heads: a gold **!** means a quest, a gold **?** means a quest to hand in, and a white **!** means news. The same dots show on the minimap.

**The village changes with your progress:**
- After the goblins: a trophy banner and bunting go up in the plaza.
- After the Berserkers: the Farmstead Folk set up camp west of the village.
- The Lich King's purple mist creeps south over the map until he falls.

**Ambient life:**
- A day/night cycle (about 6 minutes).
- Chimney smoke, chickens, grazing sheep, deer that bolt, crows that scatter, and butterflies by day.
- Five hidden treasure caches in the forest.
- Barricades and torches guard the north road into the plaza.

## Run
```bash
python -m pip install pygame-ce
python run_map.py
```

**Controls:**
- WASD / arrows: move.
- Space / J / left click: attack (in dungeons, and in the village during a raid; also breaks pots and crates).
- Shift: dash.
- H / P: potion.
- E / Enter: talk, open, read, enter portals.
- Tab / L: journal.
- Q at a lit checkpoint: leave the dungeon (back to Oakwood). Otherwise a dungeon is left by finishing it.
- R: retry.
- M: minimap.
- Esc: pause.
